What is Xilnex?
A cloud-based point-of-sale platform for multi-outlet retail and F&B businesses in Malaysia. Checkout, inventory, loyalty and staff clocking run in one system, with a single back office covering every branch. Operating since 2007, with over 30,000 installations.
Does it keep working if the internet goes down?
Yes. Terminals keep processing sales offline and queue them locally. When the connection returns, everything syncs to head-office reporting automatically, with no re-entry at the till.
Can I control pricing across every outlet from head office?
Yes. Set prices, promotions and product data once at head office and push them to selected outlets or all of them. Reporting shows which terminals have applied the change.
Is Xilnex suitable for franchise operations?
Yes. Franchisees see their own outlet performance while the franchisor sees consolidated sales and outlet comparisons across the network. See POS for retail chains & franchises.
Does it support DuitNow QR and e-wallets?
Yes, alongside card payments and third-party delivery platforms through open APIs. Payment methods are configured per outlet and every channel reports into the same daily sales figure.
